Company

A sole-trader research and development venture dedicated to exploratory innovation in physics, electrical engineering, and energy systems

The work centres on the creative optimisation and practical extension of established, tier-one scientific and engineering principles—refining, combining, and applying proven concepts from leading research rather than pursuing speculative or ungrounded claims

Activities focus on small-scale, iterative experimentation and concept development aimed at improving efficiency, performance, and novel applications within existing frameworks of energy conversion, electromagnetic systems, and related physical phenomena The approach is deliberately grounded, resource-efficient, and exploratory, prioritising rigorous evaluation of known principles to identify incremental advances and creative implementations suitable for further development
